Classes and Services

Class Types

Each class is designed to meet you where you are. Instructors offer modifications in every session. Choose what matters most to you to see the classes that fit, then tap “Save as my class” to remember your choice. It will be ready for you on the Workshops and Events request form.

Gentle Flow

A slow-paced class with basic poses and plenty of breathing cues. Ideal for beginners, older adults, and anyone returning to movement.

Vinyasa

A flowing class that links movement with breath to build strength and focus. Our 45-minute lunch-hour option fits into a busy workday.

Restorative Yoga

A deeply relaxing class that uses bolsters and blankets to support the body in longer, restful poses. A calm way to end the week.

Chair Yoga

All poses are done seated or standing with a chair for support. This class is a comfortable option for older adults and people with limited mobility.

Private Sessions

One-on-one sessions give you personal attention at your own pace. They are a good fit if you are brand new to yoga, recovering from an injury, or working toward a specific goal. Sessions are 60 minutes and can be scheduled at the studio or online.
