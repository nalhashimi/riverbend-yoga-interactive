About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Meet Our Instructors

Maya Torres, Lead Instructor

Maya has taught yoga for twelve years and holds a 500-hour teaching certification. She specializes in Gentle Flow and Chair Yoga and loves helping first-time students feel at ease.

Daniel Brooks, Vinyasa Instructor

Daniel discovered yoga as a busy software engineer looking to manage stress. He now teaches energizing Vinyasa classes, including our popular lunch-hour sessions.

Ruth Okafor, Restorative Yoga Instructor

Ruth is a certified yoga therapist with a background in physical therapy. Her Restorative classes focus on rest, recovery, and gentle support for the body.

Accessibility Accommodations

  • Step-free entrance, elevator access, and an accessible restroom
  • Chairs, blocks, straps, and bolsters available in every class at no charge
  • Instructors trained to offer seated and standing alternatives for every pose
  • Reserved mat spaces near the door for students who need easy access

If you have a specific need, please let us know before class so we can help.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive 10 minutes early, especially for your first class.
  • Cancel at least 12 hours ahead to keep your class credit.
  • Silence phones and keep the studio a quiet, calm space.
  • Talk with your doctor before starting yoga if you have a health concern.
