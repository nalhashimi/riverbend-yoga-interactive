Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Mission

At Riverbend Yoga Studio, our mission is to make yoga calm, welcoming, and possible for every body. We help people of all ages and abilities build strength, ease stress, and find a few quiet moments in a busy week.

New to Yoga? You Belong Here

You do not need to be flexible, fit, or experienced to start. Every class offers options for different bodies, and our instructors are happy to suggest props such as blocks, straps, and chairs. Arrive ten minutes early for your first class, and we will help you get settled.

Try a Guided Breathing Practice

Take a short break and follow along with this guided breathing exercise from one of our instructors.

Featured Class: Vinyasa Strength and Flow

Vinyasa links breath and movement to build strength, balance, and focus. Poses like crow are introduced step by step with plenty of options, so you can progress at your own pace. Our 45-minute lunch-hour session is a favorite with busy professionals. Learn more about our classes and pricing.
